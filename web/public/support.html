Status for Slack support

Set your Slack status from your Apple Watch with one tap, from presets you edit on your iPhone.

Getting started

  1. Open Status on your iPhone and tap Sign in with Slack.
  2. Pick your workspace and allow the app to read and set your status.
  3. Open Status on your Apple Watch. Your presets appear within a few seconds.

Common questions

The watch says "Not connected". Open the iPhone app once with your watch nearby. The watch receives the sign-in the next time both are reachable.

My workspace won't let me sign in. Some workspaces require an admin to approve new apps. Ask your Slack admin to approve Status for Slack.

Can I use Siri? Yes. Say "Set Status to Lunch" or "Clear Status", or use the actions in the Shortcuts app.

How do I sign out? Tap Disconnect in the iPhone app. This removes the sign-in from both your iPhone and your watch.
