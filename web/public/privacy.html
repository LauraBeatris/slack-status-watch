Privacy policy

Status for Slack is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that sets your Slack status. This page explains what it does with your data. Last updated 24 October 2026.

What the app stores

When you sign in, Slack issues the app a user token that lets it read and set your own status. The token is stored in the Keychain on your iPhone and on your Apple Watch, and nowhere else. Your status presets are stored on your iPhone and copied to your watch.

What leaves your devices

The app talks to one service: Slack's API, to read and set your status. The only other network call is the sign-in callback at status-for-slack.vercel.app, a small function that exchanges Slack's one-time sign-in code for your token and hands it straight back to the app. It does not store the code, the token, or anything about you.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no server-side storage, no database.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.
  • No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Deleting your data

Tap Disconnect in the app to remove the token from both devices, or delete the app. To revoke the app's access on Slack's side, remove it under your Slack workspace's app settings.
