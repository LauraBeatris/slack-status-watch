Status for Slack is added to your workspace

Now set it up on your iPhone and Apple Watch. It takes a minute.

  1. Download Status for Slack from the App Store on your iPhone.
  2. Open it, tap Sign in with Slack, and pick your workspace.
  3. Open Status on your Apple Watch and tap a preset like Lunch. Your status changes in Slack right away, and clears itself when the time runs out.

You can also say “Set Status to Lunch” to Siri on your iPhone or watch.

This website didn't keep any access to your Slack account. The app asks you to sign in on your iPhone, and your sign-in stays on your devices. Privacy policy
