Questions

Good to know

How do I set my Slack status from my Apple Watch?

Install Status for Slack, sign in with Slack on your iPhone, then open the app on your watch and tap a preset. Your status and emoji update in Slack right away.

Can my Slack status clear itself automatically?

Yes. Every preset has an expiry, from 15 minutes to 4 hours or until a time of day, and Slack clears the status when it runs out.

Can I change my Slack status with Siri or Shortcuts?

Say “Set Status to Lunch” or “Clear Status” on your iPhone or watch, or use the actions in a Shortcuts automation, like setting Focusing when a Focus mode turns on.

Does it work with my company’s Slack workspace?

It works with any Slack workspace. Some workspaces only allow apps an admin has approved, so you may need to ask yours. You can also add it to your workspace from here first. More help
