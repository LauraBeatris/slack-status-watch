## Version 1.0

**Promotional text**

Set your Slack status from your wrist. One tap on a preset and you're at lunch, in a meeting, or heads down, and it clears itself when you're done.

**Description**

Status for Slack puts your Slack status on your Apple Watch.

Tap a preset like Lunch, In a meeting, or Focusing, and your status is set before your arm is back down. A haptic confirms it, and a checkmark shows which status is active.

PRESETS FOR YOUR DAY
Start with Lunch, In a meeting, Focusing, Away, and Out of office, or make your own on your iPhone. Pick any Slack emoji, write the text, and reorder them however you like. Changes appear on your watch right away.

CLEARS ITSELF
Each preset can expire after a set time, from 15 minutes to 4 hours, or at a time of day like 6 pm. No more "In a meeting" at midnight.

ASK SIRI
Say "Set Status to Lunch" or "Clear Status" on your watch or iPhone, or use the actions in Shortcuts to change your status from any automation.

STAYS IN SYNC
If you set your status from Slack on your computer, the watch shows it when you open the app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Sign in once with Slack. Your sign-in is kept in the Keychain on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and your watch talks to Slack directly. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

Requires a Slack workspace. Some workspaces need an admin to approve new apps.

Status for Slack is an independent app and is not made by or affiliated with Slack Technologies.

**Notes**

Status for Slack sets the user's own Slack status from Apple Watch presets.

ABOUT THE AUTHENTICATION CODE
Sign-in uses Slack OAuth. Slack's sign-in page asks for a confirmation code that Slack emails to the account. That step belongs to Slack and the app can't skip it, so build 4 adds a demonstration mode that needs no account, password or code.

TO REVIEW WITH THE DEMONSTRATION MODE (RECOMMENDED)
1. Open Status on the iPhone and tap "Try the demo". The app connects to a sample workspace and shows the presets.
2. On the iPhone, tap a preset to edit its emoji, text or expiry, or add, reorder and delete presets. Changes sync to the watch.
3. Open Status on the paired Apple Watch. It lists the presets. If it says "Not connected", tap "Try the demo" on the watch.
4. Tap a preset such as Lunch. A haptic confirms it and a checkmark marks the active preset. Scroll down and tap "Clear status" to clear it.
5. Siri: say "Set Status to Lunch" or "Clear Status".
6. "Exit demo" on either device returns to the sign-in screen.

The demonstration mode runs the same screens and the same code as a real workspace. The only difference is that the status is kept on the device and is not sent to Slack.

OPTIONAL: A LIVE SLACK WORKSPACE
- With your own Slack account: tap "Sign in with Slack" and use any Slack account, or create a free workspace at slack.com/get-started. Slack emails its confirmation code to your own address. Presets you tap on the watch then change that account's real Slack status.
- With the demo account above: tap "Sign in with Slack" and enter the workspace URL statusforslackreview.slack.com. Slack emails its confirmation code to the demo account's inbox, which is why the demonstration mode exists.

PRIVACY
The app requests only the users.profile:read and users.profile:write Slack scopes. The token is stored in the Keychain on the iPhone and Apple Watch. There are no app accounts, no analytics and no tracking.
